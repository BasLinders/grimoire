ATTACK ROLL MATHEMATICS
------------------------
To-hit roll: d20 + attack bonus vs target AC
  Attack bonus = ability modifier + proficiency bonus (+ magic bonus)

Damage per Round (DPR) formula:
  DPR = hit_probability * average_damage_on_hit
      + crit_probability * extra_crit_damage

Where:
  hit_probability = (21 - AC + attack_bonus) / 20, clamped [0.05, 0.95]
  crit_probability = 0.05 (1/20) for standard 20 crit range
  extra_crit_damage = average of one set of damage dice (not modifiers)

Example: Fighter (level 5, +5 attack, longsword 1d8+3) vs AC 15:
  hit_probability = (21 - 15 + 5) / 20 = 11/20 = 55%
  average_on_hit = 4.5 + 3 = 7.5
  crit_extra = 4.5 / 2 = 2.25   (one extra d8 averaged)
  DPR = 0.55 * 7.5 + 0.05 * 2.25 = 4.125 + 0.1125 ≈ 4.24

With Extra Attack (two attacks per action):
  DPR = 2 * 4.24 ≈ 8.47

SPELL ATTACK DPR EXAMPLE
--------------------------
Fireball (8d6, DC 15 Dex save for half):
  Average damage = 28 (8 * 3.5)
  Against DC 15 with Dex +2: P(fail) = (15 - 2) / 20 = 65%
  DPR per target = 0.65 * 28 + 0.35 * 14 = 18.2 + 4.9 = 23.1

Upcast: each additional slot level = +1d6 (+3.5 damage) per target.

CONCENTRATION PROBABILITY
--------------------------
When concentrating and taking damage, Constitution saving throw DC = max(10, half damage).
With Constitution modifier +2:
  Against DC 10: P(success) = min(95%, (11 + modifier) / 20)
  At +2 Con: P(success vs DC 10) = 13/20 = 65%
  At +4 Con (War Caster or Resilient): P(success vs DC 10) = 15/20 = 75%

War Caster or Resilient (Con) advantage on concentration saves:
  P(success with advantage) = 1 - (1 - p)^2

BOUNDED ACCURACY
-----------------
5e uses "bounded accuracy": bonuses stay small (+2 to +11 range for attack).
The d20 variance is intentionally dominant to keep all levels relevant.
Key implication: higher-level characters don't outscale lower-level content
as aggressively as in earlier editions.

At level 20 with maxed stat and proficiency (+6 + +6 = +12 attack bonus):
  Hit chance vs AC 20: (21 - 20 + 12) / 20 = 65%
  Hit chance vs AC 25: (21 - 25 + 12) / 20 = 40%

HEALING EFFICIENCY
-------------------
Healing is generally less efficient than preventing damage in 5e.
Healing in combat: most effective at low HP (prevents death saves).
Cure Wounds (1st level): 1d8 + spellcasting modifier, avg ~6-10 HP.
  Healing is best saved for bringing downed characters up (1 HP minimum).
Mass Cure Wounds (5th level): 3d8 + modifier to up to 6 creatures.

LONG REST DEPENDENCY
---------------------
At full 6-8 encounter adventuring day:
  1/3 of resources typically spent per 2-encounter block.
Avoiding long rests stretches resource pressure — key for tension.

SHORT REST RESOURCES
---------------------
Classes that benefit most from short rests:
  Warlock: regains all spell slots on short rest (very short slot list)
  Fighter: Second Wind (1d10 + level HP) and Action Surge
  Monk: Ki points fully restored on short rest

POPULATION STATISTICS FOR ENCOUNTER BALANCE
---------------------------------------------
Expected party HP at level 5 (4 characters, d8 hit die class avg):
  HP per character: 8 + (4 * 5) + (5 * Con modifier)
  With +2 Con: 8 + 20 + 10 = 38 HP each; party total ~152 HP
  With +3 Con: 8 + 20 + 15 = 43 HP each; party total ~172 HP

Deadly encounter: adjusted XP >= deadly threshold per character.
Rule of thumb: an encounter can deal up to ~50% of party total HP
as a "hard" encounter without being party-wipe threatening on average dice.

STANDARD DEVIATION OF MULTI-DICE ROLLS
----------------------------------------
For N dice of S sides: σ = sqrt(N * (S^2 - 1) / 12)

Examples:
  1d6:  σ = 1.71    4d6:  σ = 3.42
  1d8:  σ = 2.02    8d6:  σ = 4.83
  2d6:  σ = 2.42    8d8:  σ = 5.72
  1d20: σ = 5.77
