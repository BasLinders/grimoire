MULTIPLE MONSTER MULTIPLIER
-----------------------------
The adjusted XP accounts for the action economy advantage of multiple monsters.
Multiply total monster XP by the multiplier below based on monster count.

Monsters  | Multiplier
----------+-----------
1         | x1
2         | x1.5
3-6       | x2
7-10      | x2.5
11-14     | x3
15+       | x4

Small party (1-2 PCs): use the next higher multiplier.
Large party (6+ PCs):  use the next lower multiplier.

ENCOUNTER DIFFICULTY CALCULATION
----------------------------------
1. Sum the XP values of all monsters.
2. Apply the multiple monster multiplier to get Adjusted XP.
3. Compare Adjusted XP to the party's threshold (sum over all characters):
   - Below Easy threshold: Trivial (no resource drain).
   - Easy to Medium:       Low (minor resource drain).
   - Medium to Hard:       Moderate.
   - Hard to Deadly:       Severe (significant resource drain).
   - Above Deadly:         Potentially lethal.

DAILY ADVENTURE BUDGET
-----------------------
A typical adventuring day should consume roughly:
  6-8 medium/hard encounters, OR an equivalent combination of fewer,
  harder fights with short rests in between.
Adventurers typically get 2 short rests per long rest.

CHALLENGE RATING INTERPRETATION
---------------------------------
A monster's CR is designed so that 4 characters of equal level to the CR
should win a Medium encounter when fighting that single monster.
  CR = character level for a roughly Medium encounter (4-person party).

Rough equivalence:
  CR 0 - 1/2: suitable as fodder for any level
  CR 1/4 - 1: good for levels 1-2 in groups
  CR 2 - 5:   core threat range for levels 3-7
  CR 6 - 10:  threat range for levels 8-13
  CR 11 - 16: threat range for levels 14-17
  CR 17 - 24: threat range for levels 18-20
  CR 25+:     legendary / mythic encounters

ACTION ECONOMY NOTES
---------------------
Multiple monsters gain an inherent advantage because they can take many
actions before the party can respond (especially on first round).
This is why the XP multiplier exists: 4 wolves (CR 1/4 each = 200 XP)
hit as hard as roughly 400 XP adjusted, not 200 XP.

Key action economy concepts:
- Legendary Actions: some powerful creatures take actions on other turns.
- Lair Actions: on initiative count 20, lair can act.
- Legendary Resistance: creatures can choose to succeed on failed saves
  (limited uses per day); plan for this when designing save-heavy encounters.
- Multiattack: creatures with multiattack scale disproportionately with
  number of targets — don't clump the party.

ENCOUNTER TEMPLATES (4-PERSON PARTY)
--------------------------------------
Level 1-4 (low tier):
  Standard: 1-2 monsters near party CR, 2-4 weaker mooks
  Boss fight: 1 CR = level, 2-4 mooks at CR level-2

Level 5-10 (mid tier):
  Standard: 1-3 monsters at CR = level-1, pack support at CR level-4
  Boss fight: 1 CR = level+1 or +2, legendary actions recommended

Level 11-16 (high tier):
  Boss fights should use legendary actions and resistances.
  Environmental hazards and lair actions add tension without just adding HP.

Level 17-20 (epic tier):
  Solo boss encounters with full legendary/lair action suites.
  Consider mythic transformations for multi-phase fights.
