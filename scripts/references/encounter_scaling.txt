ENCOUNTER SCALING AND BALANCING TECHNIQUES FOR D&D 5E
=======================================================

SCALING MONSTER STATS ON THE FLY
----------------------------------
When you need a monster at a different CR than listed, use these guidelines.

Adjusting HP:
  Each +/-1 to effective CR: +/-15 HP for most monsters.
  Alternatively scale proportionally (CR 5 monster at 157 HP → CR 7 at ~187 HP).

Adjusting AC:
  CR 1-4:   AC 13-15 typical
  CR 5-10:  AC 15-17 typical
  CR 11-16: AC 17-19 typical
  CR 17+:   AC 18-22 typical

Adjusting Attack Bonus and Damage:
  CR 1-4:   +3 to +5 attack, 2d6-3d6 damage per hit
  CR 5-10:  +5 to +8 attack, 3d6-5d6 damage per hit
  CR 11-16: +8 to +10 attack, 5d8-7d8 damage per hit
  CR 17+:   +10 to +14 attack, 7d10+ damage per hit

Adjusting Save DCs:
  CR 1-4:   DC 12-14
  CR 5-10:  DC 14-17
  CR 11-16: DC 17-19
  CR 17+:   DC 19-23

QUICK SCALING FORMULA
----------------------
Effective CR of a modified monster:
  Defensive CR: based on HP and AC.
  Offensive CR: based on damage per round and attack bonus.
  Final CR: average of Defensive CR and Offensive CR.

HP to Defensive CR mapping (approximate):
  1-6 HP:    CR 0    71-85 HP:   CR 4    171-195 HP: CR 10
  7-35 HP:   CR 1/4  86-100 HP:  CR 5    196-225 HP: CR 11
  36-49 HP:  CR 1/2  101-115 HP: CR 6    226-250 HP: CR 12
  50-70 HP:  CR 1-2  116-130 HP: CR 7    251-275 HP: CR 13
             CR 3    131-145 HP: CR 8    276-300 HP: CR 14
                     146-160 HP: CR 9    301-325 HP: CR 15

Damage per Round to Offensive CR mapping (3 rounds averaged):
  0-1:    CR 0      22-26:  CR 4      62-72:   CR 11
  2-3:    CR 1/8    27-32:  CR 5      73-84:   CR 12
  4-5:    CR 1/4    33-38:  CR 6      85-97:   CR 13
  6-8:    CR 1/2    39-44:  CR 7      98-110:  CR 14
  9-14:   CR 1      45-50:  CR 8      111-130: CR 15
  15-20:  CR 2      51-56:  CR 9      131-150: CR 16
  21-26:  CR 3      57-62:  CR 10     151-170: CR 17

MINION RULES (OPTIONAL)
------------------------
Minions are low-HP monsters designed to die in one hit.
Rule of thumb: take a standard monster and give it 1 HP.
Multiply XP by 0.5 for single-attack minions (they die before acting).
Typical use: 1 standard monster + 4-6 minions as a CR-equivalent encounter.

SWARM SCALING
--------------
Swarms are single stat blocks representing many creatures.
Swarms have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, slashing.
Swarms can occupy another creature's space.
Split swarms by halving HP to represent dispersal at half health.
For CR scaling: each swarm counts as a single monster for multiplier purposes.

TERRAIN AND ENVIRONMENTAL MODIFIERS
-------------------------------------
Terrain advantages/disadvantages can effectively shift encounter difficulty by 1 tier.
Examples of dangerous terrain:
  - Difficult terrain: halves movement, especially punishing in small rooms.
  - Elevation: ranged attackers on high ground gain advantage.
  - Darkness: heavily obscured = effectively blinded.
  - Hazardous surfaces: acid pools, lava, magical darkness.

ENCOUNTER VARIETY FOR PACING
------------------------------
Not every encounter needs to be combat.
  Social encounters: use Persuasion/Deception/Intimidation DCs 10-25.
  Exploration: skill challenges, trap navigation, resource management.
  Combat: use the XP budget system above.

Recommended mix per session:
  1-2 combat encounters (medium to hard)
  1 social or exploration challenge
  1 potential boss or climactic encounter

SHORT REST RECOVERY
--------------------
Characters with hit dice recover HP on short rest.
Short rest (1 hour): spend hit dice, regain HP.
  Fighter: Second Wind (bonus action, 1d10 + fighter level HP).
  Warlock: Regains spell slots on short rest.
  Monk: Regains ki points on short rest.
Long rest (8 hours): regain all HP, half hit dice, all spell slots.

DEATH AND DYING RULES SUMMARY
-------------------------------
At 0 HP: unconscious, start making death saving throws.
  Roll d20 each turn: 10+ = success, 1-9 = failure.
  3 successes: stabilize (still unconscious).
  3 failures: dead.
  Natural 1: counts as 2 failures.
  Natural 20: regain 1 HP, regain consciousness.
  Taking damage at 0 HP: 1 failure (melee) or 2 failures (critical hit).
  Stabilized: no longer making saves, but still unconscious until 1d4 hours.
