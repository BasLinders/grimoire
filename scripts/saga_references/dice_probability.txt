DICE PROBABILITY REFERENCE FOR D&D 5E
======================================

SINGLE DIE EXPECTED VALUES
---------------------------
d4:  average 2.5  (range 1-4)
d6:  average 3.5  (range 1-6)
d8:  average 4.5  (range 1-8)
d10: average 5.5  (range 1-10)
d12: average 6.5  (range 1-12)
d20: average 10.5 (range 1-20)

ADVANTAGE AND DISADVANTAGE (d20)
---------------------------------
With advantage (roll 2d20, take highest):
  Expected value: 13.825
  Effective +bonus: roughly equivalent to +3.3 to +5.5 depending on target DC.

With disadvantage (roll 2d20, take lowest):
  Expected value: 7.175
  Effective penalty: roughly -3.3 to -5.5 depending on target DC.

Probability of rolling at least N on a d20:
  N=1:  100%   N=6:  75%   N=11: 50%   N=16: 25%
  N=2:   95%   N=7:  70%   N=12: 45%   N=17: 20%
  N=3:   90%   N=8:  65%   N=13: 40%   N=18: 15%
  N=4:   85%   N=9:  60%   N=14: 35%   N=19: 10%
  N=5:   80%   N=10: 55%   N=15: 30%   N=20:  5%

Probability of hitting AC X with attack bonus B (= P(d20 >= X - B)):
  Hit chance = max(5%, min(95%, (21 - AC + bonus) / 20))
  Note: a natural 1 always misses, natural 20 always hits.

Advantage hit probability for attack bonus B against AC X:
  p = base hit chance
  p_adv = 1 - (1 - p)^2

Disadvantage hit probability:
  p_dis = p^2

CRITICAL HIT RULES
------------------
On a natural 20 (or with certain abilities that expand the crit range):
  Roll all damage dice twice and add modifiers once.
  Example: 1d8+4 longsword crit = 2d8+4, average 13 (vs 8.5 normal).

SAVING THROWS
-------------
Saving throw DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + relevant ability modifier.
  Level 1-4:  proficiency +2
  Level 5-8:  proficiency +3
  Level 9-12: proficiency +4
  Level 13-16:proficiency +5
  Level 17-20:proficiency +6

Probability of succeeding a saving throw with modifier M against DC D:
  P(success) = max(5%, min(95%, (21 - D + M) / 20))
